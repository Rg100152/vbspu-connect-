UP State Scholarship Checklist

Standard documentary prerequisites for UP Post Matric Scholarship scheme submission:

  • ✓ Domicile Certificate (Uttar Pradesh)
  • ✓ Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS if applicable)
  • ✓ Income Certificate (Annual family income threshold as per norms)
  • ✓ High School & Intermediate Marksheets
  • ✓ Previous Year Marksheet with minimum qualifying percentage
  • ✓ Aadhaar Card linked with active NPCI bank account
  • ✓ University Fee Receipt & Allotment Letter
Note: Application submissions occur exclusively via the official UP scholarship portal (scholarship.up.gov.in). This portal does not process transactions.
